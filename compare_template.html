Definitions, limitations & sources

Definitions

Free cash flow = operating cash flow − absolute capital expenditure, using the same fiscal year. Net debt = total debt − cash and short-term investments (cash equivalents if investments are unavailable). Debt and lease coverage follow the provider.

Dilution = year-over-year change in diluted weighted-average shares. A negative value means net contraction of that average, not proof of gross buybacks. Splits, acquisitions and ADR or share-class differences need review. Stock compensation is shown separately.

Interpretation

Yahoo Finance aggregates reported data; these figures have not received a complete primary-source review. Fiscal years can end on different dates. Historical data may be restated. This page does not convert currencies or estimate missing values.

Reported FCF here is separate from InvestorAce sustainable FCF, AFFO, distributable cash flow or mid-cycle assumptions. Regime fit and the fundamental score remain separate. No scores or portfolio weights change.
