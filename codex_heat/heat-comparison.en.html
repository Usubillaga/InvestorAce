Proposed forward comparison

Both proposed paper accounts would put half in the ten largest S&P 500 holdings, in proportion to weight, and half in RSP. One would require a panic reading below 20 and the other below 17, confirmed before the close. Both would use the same 42-month minimum and euphoria exit.

The pink journal tracks the leaders of three indexes. The figures here come from the historical close-signal backtest. A forward evaluation of the mix needs its own orders recorded before execution.

Data and method

The historical equal-weight sleeve holds the original names after purchase. RSP follows its own rebalancing schedule. Historical Top 10 ranks use estimated market cap or dollar volume and can count two classes of one issuer. Costs are 0.10% per purchase or sale. Dividends are reinvested and there is no stop. Drawdown is the largest peak-to-trough drop in daily closing values.
