Historical trades and data limitations

The simulation reconstructs market breadth using the November 2026 InvestorAce universe and omits the optional CNN indicator. The ETF holdings reports were public before each signal, but some weights were up to 493 days old. The paper account requires daily holdings dated within the previous seven days.

The 63-session holding period starts at entry. Each portfolio starts the period in cash, reinvests dividends and pays 0.1% per side. The 20 starting dates overlap.
