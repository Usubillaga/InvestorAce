# Corteva after the Vylor separation

The separation closed on 1 October 2026. CTVA retained Crop Protection. Vylor received Seed and is the accounting successor, so pre-spin consolidated Corteva/EIDP statements follow Vylor. The New Corteva historical carve-out and the separate pro forma exhibit cover the business behind today’s CTVA quote. Their accounting bases differ. ## New standalone economics and model impact

The pro forma September 30 balance sheet shows USD 1,798m of cash, USD 60m short-term borrowing and USD 944m long-term debt. The corresponding historical carve-out figures were USD 1,722m, 843m and 2,372m. The full pro forma cash adjustment includes other transfers and financing adjustments in addition to the October 1 settlement. For 9M26 pro forma sales were USD 5,357m versus USD 5,536m in the historical carve-out. Continuing income after tax was USD 112m versus USD 197m historical. The pro forma exhibit sets out balance-sheet and income-statement adjustments without a corresponding statement of standalone operating cash flows. [Pro forma combined statements and Notes 2–3](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1755672/000175567226000031/newcorteva-q32026proformaf.htm)

The frozen 25 November record had no overall opportunity label, yet its profitability, balance, growth, shareholder-return and dilution components used pre-separation data. The live model now draws its reported TTM and point-in-time shares exclusively from the dated New Corteva packet. Reported operating margin and cash margin can be computed on historical carve-out terms. The model excludes predecessor beta, buybacks, leverage and annual cash history. Sustainable FCFE, DCF, reverse DCF, EPV and price limits remain unavailable pending standalone economics and comparable capital structure. When a later period appears, even with incomplete provider cash rows, the model withdraws the September financials from current scoring pending a matching issuer packet. Historical model bundles retain their original data.
