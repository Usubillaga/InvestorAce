# Booking Holdings (BKNG): deep analysis and buy-price ladder

Review date: 2026-11-02. USD, millions unless stated. Quote $148.13 (close
2026-10-30). Share figures after the **25-for-1 split** of 2 April 2026.
InvestorAce data: nightly snapshot 2026-11-01, TTM to 2026-06-30.
**Q3 2026 results are due on 3 November 2026** (Yahoo date), one day after
this review.

## Verdict first

[Wahrscheinlich] A high-quality cash compounder priced for almost no
growth. At $148 the free-cash-flow yield is 8.3% and the reverse DCF needs
only about +1.4% FCF growth a year at the model's 10.25% cost of equity,
against 13.7% a year from 2022 to 2025. The discount is not free: the
market is pricing AI agents that could book travel without Booking, and
room-night growth has slowed to the lowest guided range in years.

[Sicher] InvestorAce: **Buy 8.07**. Strong Buy up to $125.6, Buy up to
$204.9. Profile cyclical, growth, quality and value; current regime fit
(INFLATION) 55/100. All draft inputs remain REVIEW_REQUIRED.

[Wahrscheinlich] I would not buy the full position before the Q3 print:
the Q3 guide (room nights +3–5%) is the key test, and a miss is exactly the
AI-disruption story the price already fears.

## Business and latest results

- [Sicher] Q2 2026 (reported 4 August): room nights +5% to 325m, gross
  bookings +9% to $51.0bn, revenue +8% to $7.4bn, adjusted EPS $2.54
  (+15%); all four above the top of guidance.
- [Sicher] Q3 2026 guidance: room nights +3–5%, gross bookings, revenue and
  adjusted EBITDA +4–6% each, at EUR/USD 1.15 (FX costs about 1 point).
- [Wahrscheinlich] Full-year 2026: gross bookings, revenue and adjusted
  EBITDA up high single digits, adjusted EPS up low-to-mid teens.
- [Sicher] Domestic room nights grew high single digits; international
  barely grew, long-haul still hit by the Middle East conflict.
- [Wahrscheinlich] The stock was already down 22.6% year to date at the end
  of March (pre-split $4,117); concerns named were consumer sentiment,
  geopolitics and AI disruption in travel.

[Wahrscheinlich] FCF is flattered by the merchant model: customers prepay,
so growth in bookings brings working-capital inflows that reverse if
bookings shrink. Stock-based compensation is not deducted. A cautious
"owner FCF" is perhaps 10–15% lower than reported. [Vermutung]

## Risks

- [Wahrscheinlich] AI agents (search and chat platforms) booking travel
  directly would cut Booking's marketing efficiency and take rates.
- [Sicher] Slowing room nights; Q3 guide is the weakest recent range.
- [Wahrscheinlich] EU regulation (Digital Markets Act gatekeeper status),
  FX (Europe-heavy), geopolitics and long-haul travel.
- [Vermutung] FCF quality (prepayment float, SBC) overstates owner cash.
