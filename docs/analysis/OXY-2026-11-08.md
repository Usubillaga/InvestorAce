## Verdict first

[Wahrscheinlich] The downgrade is a correction of the model, not a change
in the business. The 29 October Buy rested on a cycle-average cash flow of
7.05bn that still counted cash belonging to the Berkshire preferred
holder, a provider beta of 0.25 (cost of equity 6.2%), and a higher price.
Since then, three things have changed:
- [Sicher] Commits of 5–7 November (#108/#109 and follow-ups) value OXY on
  a common-equity basis. Preferred and minority claims are deducted, and
  the cycle mean uses the same perimeter. Valuation FCF fell from 7.05bn
  to 3.32bn, and the valuation P/E rose from 10.8 to 28.4.
- [Sicher] The beta floor (0.6 instead of the reported 0.15) raised the
  cost of equity to 7.69%.
- [Sicher] The price fell 10% (62.86 → 56.38).

[Wahrscheinlich] On today's oil price, OXY earns far more than the cycle
average: Q2 2026 alone produced 3.0bn of free cash flow before working
capital. The model deliberately ignores that peak. AGENTS.md: "Commodity
peak multiples do not substitute for multi-year valuation inputs." So OXY
is **cheap only if oil stays near 100 USD**. On cycle cash flow it is
fairly priced.

[Sicher] InvestorAce now: **Hold Positive 6.67**, Buy up to $46.27
(−18%), no Strong Buy price. Regime fit (INFLATION) 78.4/100. Flags:
MIDCYCLE_PROXY, CYCLE_REVENUE_GROWTH.

## Latest results (Q2 2026, reported 5 August)

- [Sicher] Free cash flow before working capital from continuing
  operations: 3.0bn, the highest since Q3 2022. Capex 1.6bn; operating
  cash flow from continuing operations 5.09bn.
- [Sicher] Production 1,433 Mboe/d, above the top of guidance.
- [Sicher] Principal debt reduced by 1.9bn to 11.8bn (target 10bn);
  unrestricted cash about 4.2bn.
- [Sicher] Reported EPS 2.75, adjusted EPS from continuing operations 2.40.
  Midstream and marketing earned about 960m, a record.
- [Sicher] Dividend 0.28 per quarter (+8% this year).
- [Wahrscheinlich] In September some Gulf of America production was
  curtailed by a third-party pipeline constraint and maintenance. Total
  production stayed within guidance. Q3 consensus EPS is 1.37–1.45
  (sources differ).

## Portfolio changes

- [Sicher] OxyChem was sold to Berkshire Hathaway for 9.7bn in cash, closed
  2 January 2026. Legacy tort and environmental liabilities stay with
  Occidental. About 6.5bn was planned for debt repayment.
- [Wahrscheinlich] Berkshire's preferred stock (about 8.3–8.5bn, 8%
  dividend, about 0.66–0.68bn a year) stays outstanding. Redemptions are
  planned to resume from August 2029. The model uses 8,287m from the
  provider rows; the 10-Q figure was not checked here.
