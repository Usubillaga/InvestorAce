# Altria (MO): deep analysis and buy-price ladder

Review date: 2026-11-02. USD, millions unless stated. Quote $78.03 (close
2026-10-30). InvestorAce data: nightly snapshot 2026-11-01, TTM to
2026-09-30. Q3 2026 was reported on 29 October 2026; next report about
28 January 2027.

## Verdict first

[Wahrscheinlich] InvestorAce rates Altria a Buy mostly for its regime fit
and a low discount rate, not because the stock is cheap. The business is a
cash machine in slow, managed decline: cigarette volumes fall about 4% a
year, pricing keeps earnings growing a few percent, and roughly 78% of free
cash flow goes out as dividends. At $78 the price is fair for a business
that shrinks slowly. It is not a bargain, and the stock has already risen
about 13% since late August ($69.12).

[Sicher] InvestorAce: **Buy 7.93**. Strong Buy up to $51.2, Buy up to
$106.3. Profile defensive, quality and value. Current regime fit
(INFLATION) 81.5/100, RECESSION 95, STAGFLATION 85.5, GOLDILOCKS only 29.5.
The cost of equity uses the beta floor (reported beta 0.46 → 0.6, rate
7.76%).

[Wahrscheinlich] The $106 Buy ceiling is too generous. It rests on 5.4%
FCF growth taken from 2024→2025, which a business with falling volumes is
unlikely to repeat. At an 8.5% rate the no-growth value is only $64.

## Risks

- [Sicher] Structural volume decline; illicit disposable vapes take share.
- [Wahrscheinlich] Regulation (nicotine limits, flavour bans), litigation,
  excise taxes.
- [Wahrscheinlich] Rate sensitivity: the stock trades partly as a bond
  proxy; higher yields lower its value.
- [Wahrscheinlich] Smoke-free transition (NJOY, on!) has not yet shown it
  can replace cigarette profits.

## Why the model still says Buy

Valuation and quality scores are high (59.8% margin, FCF 112% of net
income, reverse DCF −2.8%), and the INFLATION regime rewards defensive
cash payers (macro 8.15/10). [Wahrscheinlich] If the regime turns to
REFLATION (fit 40) or GOLDILOCKS (29.5), the label likely drops to Hold
Positive without any change in the business.
