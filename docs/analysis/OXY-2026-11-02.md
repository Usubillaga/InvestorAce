## Why the model says Buy

[Sicher] NGV $115.92 = FCF/share $7.05 ÷ ~6.1%. Three inputs drive it:

1. [Sicher] Mid-cycle FCF 7,051 is the FY2022–2025 mean (12,313 / 6,612 /
   5,176 / 4,105). It contains 2022 at ~$95 WTI. [Wahrscheinlich] The
   earlier years were reported with OxyChem consolidated; OxyChem was sold
   on 2 January 2026.
2. [Sicher] `fcf / shares` ignores the 680/yr preferred dividend and the
   ~8,925 call in 2029.
3. [Wahrscheinlich] ~6.1% cost of equity (reported beta 0.245, floored at
   0.6) is too low for a commodity producer with this capital structure.

[Wahrscheinlich] Single corrections: rate 10% → ~$71; FCF 4,800 at $65
WTI → ~$79; all three (4,120 to common at 10%) → ~$41. The INFLATION regime
(fit 97.2) and the low reported P/E (8.8x on war-price earnings) add to the
Buy. AGENTS.md already notes that commodity peak multiples do not substitute
for multi-year valuation inputs.
