### Conditional research valuation

[Vermutung] The base assumes oil 555 thousand barrels/day, NGL 380 thousand
and gas 4.475 Bcf/day for 365 days, six Mcf/BOE and one Mcf/MMBtu as a gas-price
proxy. WTI is 70 and Henry Hub 3.25; realized oil is 99% of WTI, NGL 27.5%
and gas 55% of Henry Hub. LOE/GPT/G&A are 4.75/3.45/1.30 per BOE, with SBC
included in G&A. Production tax is 7% of product sales, exploration 60 and
marketing margin 90. D&A is 11/BOE; cash tax is 23% of positive EBIT.
Annual reinvestment allowances are 5,500 operating capex, 300 resource access
and 100 external midstream/JV funding. These sustainable allowances need review.

This produces baseline FCFF of 5,243.150874. Incremental after-tax synergies
are assumed at 300 in year 1 and 500 from year 2, including the terminal year.
The ten-year DCF uses 10.5% WACC and zero other/terminal growth. With constant
cash from year 2, its base reproduces as:

```text
EV = (5,243.150874 + 500) / 0.105 + (300 - 500) / 1.105
claims = 11,388 - 950 + 40 + 1,223 = 11,701
equity/share = (EV - claims) / 1,160 = 36.909206 USD
```

debt or investment-portfolio addon is applied. The bear/base/bull results from
