reported US GAAP revenue of EUR32,667.3m, the new midpoint is:

`100 * (44,500 / 32,667.3 - 1) = 36.22184876007506%`.

used a rounded 35%. Therefore the company-guidance effect is +1.53058pp;

growth proxy, not a normalized cash-flow-per-share growth forecast.

## Cash-flow bridge and normalization

ASML defines reported FCF as OCF minus purchases of property, plant and
equipment **and** intangible assets. All numbers below are EUR millions.

| Period | OCF | PPE purchases | Intangible purchases | Calculated issuer-basis FCF |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| FY2025 | 12,658.5 | 1,573.6 | 57.6 | 11,027.3 |
| 9M2025 | 1,248.2 | 1,125.7 | 35.1 | 87.4 |
| 9M2026 | 11,315.0 | 1,115.1 | 140.4 | 10,059.5 |
| TTM to 2026-09-27: FY2025 - 9M2025 + 9M2026 | 22,725.3 | 1,563.0 | 162.9 | 20,999.4 |

The earlier monitoring calculation of EUR10,199.9m for 9M2026 subtracted only
PPE. It is an OCF-minus-PPE subtotal, not issuer-defined FCF. Intangibles reduce
it by EUR140.4m to EUR10,059.5m.

Q3 OCF of EUR11,797.6m includes EUR7,009.0m of changes in assets/liabilities;
the 9M adjustment is EUR236.2m versus a EUR6,614.2m outflow in 9M2025. Timing,
customer advances, working-capital requirements and additional capacity spending
must be reconciled over comparable full periods. Q3 also has a EUR509.7m
non-operating equity-investment gain, reversed in the OCF reconciliation by
EUR508.8m. It must not be deducted from OCF again.

The EUR20,999.4m reported TTM proxy is recorded separately from the existing
EUR10,000m `fcf` estimate. Neither the TTM proxy nor a quarterly annualization
is substituted for normalized sustainable FCF. The existing estimate has no
verified financial period or FCFE/FCFF contract; `NORMALIZATION REVIEW` is
shown using the existing report mechanism. No false financial date is assigned
to that estimate merely because the new guidance is dated.

## Category review and completion condition

Growth/profitability evidence improves, but the existing g/p values are already
9.5/9.5 and their original category rubric is not established by this release.
Cash conversion needs the timing reconciliation; balance and payout require
consistent debt/share bases. Existing subjective subscores remain pending that
review. Higher regime fit cannot increase the fundamental score.

Resolve normalization by reconciling advances and working capital over FY2025,
9M2025 and 9M2026; verify maintenance/growth capex (including intangibles),
the incremental expansion plan and current ordinary shares. Then declare an
explicit annual cash-flow/rate contract and perform a controlled revaluation.
The 15:00 issuer call is a follow-up source for quantified capacity/capex
information; the video interview is not that investor call.
