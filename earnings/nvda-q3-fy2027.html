Why the stock rose after hours

What the market liked

  • Revenue $115.8B vs about $109.0B expected; adjusted EPS $2.68 vs about $2.47–2.49.
  • Q4 outlook $130B ±3%, without any China data-center compute revenue.
  • Vera Rubin NVL72 shipping and generally available on major clouds; "fastest product ramp" in company history.
  • $28.1B returned to shareholders in the quarter; buyback authorization raised by $150B.

What it does not answer

  • No permanent CEO: Jensen Huang resigned on 11–12 Nov amid an independent board investigation (press-reported).
  • Gross margin guided down to 71–72% in Q4 from 74.8%, as Rubin ramps.
  • Q4 includes an extra week, which flatters the sequential growth.
  • Agreement to acquire Hugging Face announced; price and terms not found†.
