Why the stock barely moved

What held the stock up

  • TPV $18.3bn, +77% y/y (+68% in constant currency): volume growth is accelerating, not slowing.
  • Record net income $68.0M (+31%) and diluted EPS $0.23 vs $0.21–0.22 expected.
  • Operating profit / gross profit 58%, the best since 4Q23: OPEX fell 11% q/q.
  • Adjusted free cash flow $99M in the quarter, 146% of net income†; $182M over nine months.

What capped it

  • Revenue $398.0M, flat q/q ($399.7M in Q2) and about 1–2% below consensus.
  • Mexico: revenue +64% but gross profit −2% y/y and −17% q/q. Growth bought with price.
  • FY operating-profit growth guided to the low end of 27.5–32.5%, while TPV goes to the top end.
  • Part of the OPEX cut is temporary per management, so the 58% margin may not hold.
