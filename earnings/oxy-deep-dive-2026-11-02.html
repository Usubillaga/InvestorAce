06 · The debate
What each side gets right
The bull case is mostly about the company; the bear case is mostly about the price you pay and the commodity.

Why bulls own it

  • Deleveraging done fast: $20.4B → $11.8B principal in six months; interest falls ~$630M/yr vs 2025.
  • Cash machine at war prices: Q2 FCF $3.0B, best since Q3 2022; production beat the top of guidance and FY guide was raised to 1,423–1,453 Mboed.
  • Self-help that doesn't need oil: >$4B annual cash-flow uplift targeted by 2030, ~85% claimed achievable at lower prices; base decline 25% → ~20%; CO₂ pilots +45% EUR in the Permian.
  • Unhedged from 2027: the only collar (100 kb/d, $55 × $75.89) expires at year-end — full torque if the risk premium sticks.
  • Aligned owners: Berkshire at 26.7% plus preferred plus warrants; CEO bought weeks into the job.

Why bears won't

  • No margin of safety: the price already discounts ~$69–73 long-run WTI — the curve, not a discount to it.
  • Headline beta: −3.6% today on peace-talk reports; Q2 showed what a ceasefire does (OXY ~$65 → ~$49).
  • Capital-return ceiling: big buybacks wait until the $8.9B preferred call in Aug 2029 — the windfall goes to creditors and Berkshire first.
  • Contingent claims: $2.4B Tronox tax exposure plus up to $1.9B of environmental cost beyond the $1.85B already accrued — ~$4/share if both crystallise.
  • Execution history: Stratos DAC was due in late 2024 and is still only targeted for start-up by end-2026; Gulf output just lost ~34 Mboed to a third-party pipeline.
  • Dilution: 101.8M warrants (+10%) — Berkshire's tranche moves into the money above $59.59.

How the numbers were built
  • [Wahrscheinlich] FCF (before working capital, before preferred dividends) of $4.8B at $65 WTI for the 2027 run-rate: FY2025 continuing-ops FCF ~$3.2–3.3B at ~$65 oil plus management's >$1.2B 2026 improvement at flat prices.
  • [Wahrscheinlich] Oil sensitivity $0.23B FCF per $1/bbl WTI per year (≈705 kb/d oil, after cash tax, NGL co-movement). Check: model gives $3.03B for Q2-26 vs $3.02B reported; $1.48B for Q1-26 vs $1.75B reported.
  • [Wahrscheinlich] Plan uplift at full delivery: $5.0B (2027) → $6.4B (2030) at $65, i.e. the >$4B pre-tax target taxed at ~22%; scenarios credit 30% / 60% / 100%.
  • [Sicher] Preferred valued as a claim of ~$8.5B (8% coupons to Aug-2029 + 105% call, discounted at 10%); warrants by treasury method (1,014M diluted shares at ~$61.70).
  • [Wahrscheinlich] 10% cost of equity — cross-checked: at the Dec-2025 low ($38.80) the same model implies ~$62–65 mid-cycle oil, roughly the pre-war consensus.
  • [Vermutung] Scenario probabilities and the 50/50 Tronox outcome. Price ~$61.70 is an estimate from the 30 Oct close and the −3.6% intraday move at 11:02 ET on 2 Nov.
  • Not modelled: WES stake value beyond its cash distributions, Stratos/45Q economics, a production-growth case (~2% CAGR) management also outlines.
