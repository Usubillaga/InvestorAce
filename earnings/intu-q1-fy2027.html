Why the stock fell 12.8%

What went right

  • Revenue $4.354B (+12%) vs about $4.31B expected†; non-GAAP EPS $2.91 vs about $2.70 expected†.
  • GAAP operating income +63% to $868M†; GAAP EPS $2.59†.
  • Online Ecosystem revenue +20%†; QBO Advanced and Intuit Enterprise Suite +41%†.
  • Full-year non-GAAP EPS range kept at $22.88–23.12, now including stock-based compensation; $1.5B bought back in the quarter†.

What the market sold

  • Full-year revenue range lowered to about $23.08–23.37B (+8% to 9%) from $23.28–23.51B†.
  • Credit Karma growth guided to 3–7%†; refund-transfer processing moves in-house, about $30M hit in Q2†.
  • Mailchimp sold to Bending Spoons for about $3.68B ($3.31B cash, $368M stock) vs about $12B paid; a write-down of about $8.3B is press-reported†.
  • Q2 revenue guided to about $4.93–4.95B, +6% to 7% (one source)†. Growth keeps slowing while AI tax and bookkeeping rivals get cheaper.
