Why the stock jumped

What the market bought

  • Revenue $736M vs about $650M expected and the company's own $700M outlook.
  • Adjusted EPS $0.33 vs $0.25 expected.
  • 15% workforce reduction and buyback raised to $1bn.
  • Co-founder Dave Pickles returns to the business.

What the rally ignores

  • Revenue −0.5% y/y; about 3 pts came from one non-recurring partner.
  • Adjusted EBITDA $266M, margin 36% vs 43%.
  • GAAP net income $38M, EPS $0.08, after a $44.3M restructuring charge.
  • Q4 guide of at least $755M is about 11% below last year.
